# PX Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in the PX ecosystem a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
Positive behavior: empathy, kindness, respect for differing views, accepting constructive feedback, focusing on community best.
Unacceptable behavior: sexualized language, trolling, insults, harassment, publishing private info, other inappropriate professional conduct.

## Enforcement
Report unacceptable behavior to founder@priprostudios.site. All complaints reviewed promptly and fairly.
